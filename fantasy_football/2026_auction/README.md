# 2026 Movers and Shakers Auction Model

Custom auction framework for a 10-team ESPN salary-cap league with a $500 budget.

## League-specific scoring notes
- 1 QB / 2 RB / 2 WR / 1 TE / 1 FLEX / 1 DST / 1 K / 7 bench
- Full PPR
- 1 point per pass completion
- 6 points per passing TD
- 1 point per 25 passing yards
- -2 per interception
- Passing yardage and explosive-TD bonuses
- Rushing/receiving yardage, TD and milestone bonuses
- No keepers

The unusual 1-point-per-completion rule materially raises the VOR and auction value of high-volume quarterbacks, especially Joe Burrow, Josh Allen, Drake Maye, Dak Prescott and Jared Goff relative to standard 1QB auction boards.

## Workbook
The companion spreadsheet contains:
- Top 300 board
- Custom projected points/VOR
- Model price and hard max bid
- Market comparison and edge
- Target/fade/risk tags
- Draft strategy budget buckets
- Live auction tracker with remaining-budget formulas
- Current source list

Source anchors include FantasyPros 2026 PPR consensus and QB projections, RotoWire 2026 auction values/sleepers/busts, Yahoo/Justin Boone Top 300, ESPN QB rankings, and Draft Sharks PPR rankings.

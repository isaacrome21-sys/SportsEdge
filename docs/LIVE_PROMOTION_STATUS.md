# LIVE PROMOTION STATUS

Current status: **BLOCKED / UNRUN**

Reason: runtime and acquisition architecture exist, but no sport/market live predictive model has completed point-in-time historical replay plus forward shadow validation.

The following are not yet proven:

- live calibration by market
- Brier/log-loss improvement versus contemporaneous no-vig live market
- game-clustered confidence intervals
- live CLV under realistic quote latency
- post-vig ROI under realistic acceptance/slippage
- edge-bin monotonicity
- live mode parity under identical normalized snapshots
- durable high-frequency archive coverage

No live model may be promoted or emit an official governed live bet until a separately frozen live promotion policy is satisfied with real out-of-sample evidence.

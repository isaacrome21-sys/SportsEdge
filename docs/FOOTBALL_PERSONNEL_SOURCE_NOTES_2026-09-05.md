# Football Personnel Source Notes — 2026-09-05

Focused source mapping for the five daily checks requested by SportsEdge.

## QB status
Primary: official team availability/depth information. Supporting: PFF, Sports Info Solutions, SIC Score, Next Gen Stats, CBS returning-snap continuity where useful.

## OL / DL depth
Primary: official depth charts. Supporting: PFF, CFBDepth, TWO·DEEP, SIS Total Points and ESPN Analytics trench metrics.

## Snap / usage changes
Primary structured sources: PFF and SIS. Supporting: RotoWire, Lineups.com for NFL, CBS returning-snap percentages for early-season CFB continuity. SportsEdge should compute rolling week-over-week deltas where raw snap tables are available.

## Secondary availability
Primary: official status/depth. Supporting: PFF, CFBDepth, TWO·DEEP, SIS, SIC Score and Next Gen Stats coverage-role context.

## Meaningful unit mismatches
Use PFF/SIS/CFBDepth/TWO·DEEP as quantitative or structural context. Editorial mismatch posts remain low-priority commentary and never become votes.

## Integration rule
Every material item is stored as provenance-bearing context with `model_p=false` unless a versioned, validated SportsEdge transform explicitly converts that information into a model feature. Source agreement cannot create or boost a betting recommendation.

# SportsEdge Football — Kicker and Defense Market Specs

These remain shared-path read-outs. Kicker markets require Engine C. Defensive player markets require defender participation in Engine B and defender identity attribution in Engine A; if that attribution is not implemented they stay `NO_ENGINE` rather than using a detached prop model.

## Field Goals Made
### 1. READ-OUT DEFINITION
For kicker k on path i, `FGM_i=count(FG attempts by k resolved made by C)`. Each attempt is generated by A's field position/down/score/clock state and coaching decision policy; make probability is distance/environment-specific. A+C, with B only for kicker participation if roster substitution is modeled there.
### 2. REQUIRED INPUTS
Kicker active identity/status (free, TTL 10-30m game day, block), historical make curve by distance (free basic/paid richer, TTL 7d), coaching fourth-down/FG decision tendencies (7d), long snap/holder issues when known (news, 30m; degrade/block if material), wind/gust/roof at kick time (60/30m, block if material unresolved), field/surface/altitude static, quote 30-90s.
### 3. HOLD AND THRESHOLD
NFL 8-14%; P4 10-18%; G5/FCS 15-25% and often not offered. `b=2.0pp, lambda=0.80, z=1.60`.
### 4. SETTLEMENT RULES
Official made FGs only; nullified attempts excluded. OT included if book full-game player-prop rules include it. Fake FGs do not count unless official attempt. Inactive kicker void per book.
### 5. VALIDATION PLAN
>=1,000 kicker-game opportunities and >=150 challenger bets; 2+ seasons preferred because attempts are sparse. Validate attempt count and distance-binned make probabilities separately, then prop reliability/CLV.
### 6. STATE TRANSITIONS
NO_ENGINE if C lacks explicit attempt/make model; INPUT_MISSING for unresolved kicker or material wind/roof; ENGINE_BLOCKED if FGs fail scoring reconciliation; PRICED then BET/PASS.
### 7. CORRELATION CLUSTER
`KICK_TREE:<team>` + game scoring; correlated with team total, red-zone failures, spread/game script, longest FG and kicking points.

## Kicking Points
### 1. READ-OUT DEFINITION
`KP_i=3*FGM_i+1*XPM_i` under standard scoring, with any book-specific treatment of unusual conversion plays encoded explicitly. A+C.
### 2. REQUIRED INPUTS
Union of FG and XP inputs: kicker identity, FG curve, TD distribution from A, XP make curve, weather/roof, coaching 2PT decisions. Unresolved kicker blocks.
### 3. HOLD AND THRESHOLD
NFL 8-14%; P4 10-18%; G5/FCS 15-25%. `b=2.0pp, lambda=0.80, z=1.60`.
### 4. SETTLEMENT RULES
Official kicker scoring points from made FGs and XPs; 2PT conversions are not kicker points unless player personally scores under an unusual market definition. OT per book.
### 5. VALIDATION PLAN
>=1,000 opportunities, >=150 bets; jointly validate TD->XP opportunity and FG opportunity distributions, then CLV.
### 6. STATE TRANSITIONS
INPUT_MISSING if kicker/2PT policy materially unresolved; ENGINE_BLOCKED if kicker points do not reconcile to scoring events; PRICED then BET/PASS.
### 7. CORRELATION CLUSTER
`KICK_TREE` + team scoring; very correlated with FG made, XP made, team total and TD count.

## Extra Points Made
### 1. READ-OUT DEFINITION
`XPM_i=count(successful one-point kick tries by k)`. Opportunities arise from TD events and coaching decisions in C. A+C.
### 2. REQUIRED INPUTS
Kicker active status, XP accuracy, team TD distribution, coaching 2PT tendency by score/time, weather/roof. Quote TTL 30-90s.
### 3. HOLD AND THRESHOLD
NFL 8-14%; P4 10-18%; G5/FCS 15-25%. `b=2.0pp, lambda=0.80, z=1.60`.
### 4. SETTLEMENT RULES
Only made one-point kick tries. 2PT attempts not XP made. Nullified tries excluded; OT per book.
### 5. VALIDATION PLAN
>=1,000 opportunities, >=150 bets; validate XP opportunity and conversion separately; CLV.
### 6. STATE TRANSITIONS
NO_ENGINE if C cannot model post-TD choice/XPs; INPUT_MISSING for kicker uncertainty; ENGINE_BLOCKED on TD/XP reconciliation; PRICED then BET/PASS.
### 7. CORRELATION CLUSTER
`KICK_TREE` + `TD_TREE`; direct dependence on team touchdowns.

## Player Sacks
### 1. READ-OUT DEFINITION
For defender d, `SACK_i=sum(official full/partial sack credit assigned to d)` from pass-play pressure outcomes. Team sack event is generated in A, defender credit allocated using B participation/pressure role. A+B.
### 2. REQUIRED INPUTS
Defender active/snap/pass-rush participation (NFL official/paid tracking, TTL 10m after inactives; CFB mixed/paid 30-60m, block if unresolved), alignment/pass-rush share/pressure rate (paid preferable, 24h), opposing QB dropbacks/sack susceptibility/OL injuries (24h/30m), score script, quote TTL. CFB defender stat feeds can be inconsistent; trustworthy settlement source required.
### 3. HOLD AND THRESHOLD
NFL 10-18%; P4 12-22%; G5/FCS 15-30% when offered. `b=2.5pp, lambda=0.90, z=1.65`.
### 4. SETTLEMENT RULES
Official sack credit, including half-sacks if sportsbook/stat provider uses fractional stat; no sack on aborted/no-play penalties unless official. OT per book. Inactive void by book.
### 5. VALIDATION PLAN
>=2,000 player-game opportunities and >=250 bets; multiple seasons. Validate team sack generation then defender allocation; Brier/log loss/reliability and CLV.
### 6. STATE TRANSITIONS
NO_ENGINE if defender identity allocation absent; INPUT_MISSING if defender snap/rush role unresolved; ENGINE_BLOCKED if player sack credits do not reconcile with team sack totals/provider semantics; PRICED then BET/PASS.
### 7. CORRELATION CLUSTER
`DEFENSE_TREE:<team>` + opponent `QB_PASS_TREE`; correlated with team sacks, QB attempts/yards, QB rush/scramble and turnovers.

## Tackles + Assists
### 1. READ-OUT DEFINITION
For defender d, `TA_i=sum(tackle_credit + assist_credit)` assigned to d on eligible plays under the bound stats-provider semantics. A generates tackle opportunity and ball location; B assigns defenders present/role and credit distribution. A+B.
### 2. REQUIRED INPUTS
Defender active/snap/position/alignment (paid tracking strongly preferred, TTL 10-60m), opponent play volume/run/pass distribution, tackle opportunity by position/zone, stat-crew/provider identity and historical assist conventions (must be known), injury/depth changes, quote TTL. Missing provider semantics or defender participation blocks.
### 3. HOLD AND THRESHOLD
NFL 10-18%; P4 12-22%; G5/FCS 15-30% and often unsupported. `b=2.8pp, lambda=0.90, z=1.70` due attribution uncertainty.
### 4. SETTLEMENT RULES
Must match sportsbook stats provider exactly; solo+assists definitions vary in practical feeds. Nullified plays excluded. OT per book. Postgame stat corrections require delayed settlement finalization window before validation lock.
### 5. VALIDATION PLAN
>=3,000 opportunities and >=300 bets preferred; multi-season, provider-specific. Validate team tackle-opportunity totals, positional allocation, crew effects and correction rate. CLV + Brier/log loss; ROI not promotion criterion.
### 6. STATE TRANSITIONS
NO_ENGINE until defender tackle-credit attribution exists; INPUT_MISSING on snap/role/provider ambiguity; ENGINE_BLOCKED on reconciliation/provider mismatch; PRICED then BET/PASS.
### 7. CORRELATION CLUSTER
`DEFENSE_TREE`; correlated with opponent play volume, rush attempts, completions and game script. Multiple defenders' tackle props are not independent because they compete for credit.

## Player Interceptions
### 1. READ-OUT DEFINITION
For defender d, `INTD_i=count(pass interceptions credited to d)`. A generates QB interception event; B/coverage-role layer assigns defender identity conditional on coverage/alignment. A+B.
### 2. REQUIRED INPUTS
Defender active/snap/coverage role, QB target/depth/INT distribution, opponent receiver usage, coverage scheme, defender ball-production prior. Paid tracking helpful. Participation unresolved blocks.
### 3. HOLD AND THRESHOLD
NFL 12-22%; P4 15-25%; G5/FCS 18-32%. `b=3.0pp, lambda=0.95, z=1.70`.
### 4. SETTLEMENT RULES
Official defensive interceptions only; 2PT interception generally excluded from standard defensive INT stats; nullified plays excluded; OT per book.
### 5. VALIDATION PLAN
Rare event: >=4,000 player-game opportunities and >=300 bets; several seasons. Validate team INT rate before defender assignment; low-probability calibration and CLV.
### 6. STATE TRANSITIONS
NO_ENGINE if defender INT identity not modeled; INPUT_MISSING on role uncertainty; ENGINE_BLOCKED if player INT sum exceeds team INT or rule semantics fail; PRICED then usually PASS.
### 7. CORRELATION CLUSTER
`DEFENSE_TREE` + opponent `QB_PASS_TREE`; correlated with QB interceptions, team turnovers, defensive TD and negative passing efficiency.

## Team Sacks
### 1. READ-OUT DEFINITION
`TS_i=count(official opponent dropbacks ending in team sack)` with provider fractional/team semantics normalized to market rule. A.
### 2. REQUIRED INPUTS
Opponent dropbacks, OL/QB status, pressure/pass-rush efficiency, score script, weather/surface. Individual defender status can degrade through team pressure mixture; if multiple top rushers unresolved and effect material, INPUT_MISSING/DEGRADED according to explicit mixture.
### 3. HOLD AND THRESHOLD
NFL 8-14%; P4 10-18%; G5/FCS 12-22%. `b=2.0pp, lambda=0.80, z=1.60`.
### 4. SETTLEMENT RULES
Official team sacks; accepted penalty/no-play treatment follows stats provider; OT per book.
### 5. VALIDATION PLAN
>=1,000 team-game opportunities, >=150 bets; team sack count calibration and CLV. One season can give pooled signal but not team-specific certainty.
### 6. STATE TRANSITIONS
PRICED if A models sack outcomes; NO_ENGINE otherwise; INPUT_MISSING for material unresolved QB/OL/pass-rush state; ENGINE_BLOCKED on dropback/sack reconciliation; BET/PASS after gate.
### 7. CORRELATION CLUSTER
`DEFENSE_TREE` + opponent `QB_PASS_TREE`; correlated with QB rush, pass yards/attempts, team turnovers and defensive scoring.

## Team Turnovers
### 1. READ-OUT DEFINITION
For defense/team d, `TO_i=count(opponent possessions ending in recoverable turnover credited as giveaway: interceptions + lost fumbles, with downs not counted unless market says takeaways broadly)`. A.
### 2. REQUIRED INPUTS
QB INT probability, fumble rates by rush/sack/reception event, recovery model, pressure, weather/surface, ball-security injuries/roles. TTL 24h, game-day participation 10-60m.
### 3. HOLD AND THRESHOLD
NFL 8-14%; P4 10-18%; G5/FCS 12-22%. `b=2.0pp, lambda=0.80, z=1.60`.
### 4. SETTLEMENT RULES
Official takeaways/giveaways according to market wording; turnover on downs excluded from standard turnover stats; muffed punts/kick returns count if official team turnover and market includes all team turnovers. OT per book.
### 5. VALIDATION PLAN
>=1,000 team-game opportunities, >=150 bets; validate INT/fumble components and recovery calibration separately, then CLV.
### 6. STATE TRANSITIONS
NO_ENGINE if fumbles/recoveries not represented; INPUT_MISSING for material QB/ball-handler uncertainty; ENGINE_BLOCKED if turnover types fail possession/stat reconciliation; PRICED then BET/PASS.
### 7. CORRELATION CLUSTER
`DEFENSE_TREE`, opponent QB/skill trees and game result. Correlated with spread, ML, defensive TD and opponent yardage/drive count.

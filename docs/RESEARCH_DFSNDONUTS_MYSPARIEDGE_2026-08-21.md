# Public model research: DFSnDonuts / MySpariEdge

Research date: 2026-08-21.

Public material reviewed from the accounts and DFSnDonuts newsletter supports the following visible workflow:

1. Candidate research sheets: BvP, recent hit trends, strikeout ladders, hits allowed, bullpen attack, pitcher NRFI records.
2. Structural matchup inputs: HardHit%, Barrel%, xwOBA/xBA, FIP/xFIP, K%, BB%, K-BB%, WHIP, opponent splits.
3. Workload inputs for pitcher props: pitch-count stability, recent form and workload/innings expectations.
4. First-inning layer: pitcher first-inning history plus opponent first-inning AVG/wOBA and first-inning runs per game.
5. Simulation/projection layer: public posts explicitly describe 10,000-simulation outputs for NRFI/YRFI and hitter projections.
6. Price layer: separate no-vig/+EV optimizer, sharp-money/market tools and line comparison.
7. Release discipline: public research posts repeatedly state that trends are research angles and should still be compared with odds, lineups, weather and line value.

Important limit: public sources do not reveal proprietary formulas, exact feature weights, training data or calibration. SportsEdge therefore implements its own math while borrowing only disclosed feature categories and workflow structure.

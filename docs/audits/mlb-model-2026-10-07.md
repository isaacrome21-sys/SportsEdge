# MLB model audit — October 7, 2026

Audited base: e92a5cc69aa74ba26b70ec8a7793c65366f025bd.
Scope: the score-compatible card runner used by PR #1844, its context means,
pitcher histories, probability identities, and economics. This is not a completed
validation of every MLB market.

## Confirmed defects repaired

- Missing pitcher K/ER/H/BB counts were silently interpreted as zero; fractional
  counts were truncated. Reject incomplete/invalid observations instead.
- Zero-out starts were excluded from starter ERA and mean workload. Include their
  earned runs and zero outs; missing ER now makes the profile unavailable instead
  of making the pitcher appear better.
- The wrapper seeded game scores with a hash containing the selected pitcher
  pools. Use game context alone so adding props does not change game probabilities.
- A pitcher's RNG depended on every other pitcher in the request. Isolate it to
  its own pool, side, and the game score identity.
- Simulation identities omitted dispersion/extras parameters. Include all three.

## Standalone pitcher pricing repair

Score-compatible resampling conditions historical starts on ER <= simulated
opponent runs. This preferentially removes high-ER starts and can also increase
outs/K through whole-row correlation. It is a support constraint, not a fitted
joint model. The regression fixture moves an unconditioned K probability of 50%
to over 80%. This demonstrates the mechanism, not the size of tonight's error.

Every pitcher result now includes the exact unconditioned empirical win/push
probabilities, conditioning delta, historical sample count, and explicit flags
showing that postseason workload and the fitted pitcher-K candidate are unused.
Standalone pitcher rows now use the canonical empirical-Bayes pitcher engine
with the native source feature builder, preserving available opponent/lineup K,
opponent-outs, umpire-BB and long-window prior inputs. Conditioned probabilities
are retained only in a nested diagnostic, never used for standalone EV. These
rows have their own engine identity, no game simulation ID, and no SGP eligibility.
The existing pitcher-card exclusion remains in effect pending postseason/workload
validation. No replacement coefficients or artificial probability caps were introduced.

## Other findings and remaining work

- This runner uses the canonical empirical-Bayes/context pitcher path, not the
  frozen fitted K candidate. 100,000 simulations do not provide 100,000 historical observations.
- Postseason pitch limits, starter removal and bullpen deployment remain
  unmodeled in standalone pitcher marginals. Available lineup K now flows through
  the native feature path; no unavailable context is fabricated.
- Context game means use recent runs for/against, starter ER/9 and a temperature
  heuristic. Starter ER/9 and team total runs allowed are not identical measures.
  Calibration of those adjustments remains unestablished by this audit.
- The push-aware EV formula is correct: p(win)*(decimal-1)-p(loss). Conditional
  non-push probability must not be displayed as unconditional win probability.
- The supplied odds and original run artifact have not been independently
  revalidated here. No fresh bet card or claimed corrected game probabilities
  were produced, and no historical evidence was backfilled.

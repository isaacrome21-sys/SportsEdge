# SportsEdge MLB Statcast-required V5 protocol — 2026-08-12

## Purpose

Statcast must be an actual predictive input, not presentation context. Existing GAME_SCORE_V4_CUTOFF_CORRECT and NRFI_V4_CUTOFF_CORRECT remain valid for their prior validation claims, but they are not Statcast models and may not be represented as such.

## Frozen chronology

- Training: 2021-2023 regular season only.
- Calibration/model selection: 2024 only.
- Final untouched historical holdout: 2025 only.
- 2026 labels are prohibited from fitting, calibration, hyperparameter selection, threshold tuning, or feature-transformer fitting.
- For each target date, all game-result state and Statcast-derived state must come from dates strictly before the target date. Same-day completed games may not update another game on the same official date.

## Point-in-time Statcast policy

Historical Savant expected-stat columns are not trusted as point-in-time snapshots because retrospective recomputation/revision cannot be ruled out from a current historical pull. V5 therefore does not train from current historical `estimated_woba_using_speedangle` or `estimated_ba_using_speedangle` values.

The expected-contact layer is frozen separately before downstream V5 fitting:

1. Pull raw official Savant batted-ball events for 2021-2023.
2. Use raw `launch_speed` and `launch_angle` as transformer inputs.
3. Define hard hit deterministically as `launch_speed >= 95` mph.
4. Define barrel deterministically from `launch_speed_angle == 6` when present.
5. Fit a frozen contact transformer on 2021-2023 only to estimate contact xBA and contact xwOBA from launch speed/angle. Outcome targets are event results with fixed contact wOBA values: out/error/FC=0.00, single=0.88, double=1.24, triple=1.56, home_run=2.00. The xBA target is 1 for single/double/triple/home_run and 0 otherwise.
6. Hash and serialize that transformer separately. Every 2024/2025/live expected-contact feature must be generated by that frozen transformer; the downstream V5 artifacts must record its SHA-256.

No current historical Savant expected-stat value may substitute for the frozen transformer output.

## Permitted sources

- Official MLB StatsAPI for schedule, identity, results, probable pitchers, lineups/rosters and non-Statcast game facts.
- Official MLB Baseball Savant / Statcast event data for raw contact inputs.
- Sportsbook data is price-only and prohibited from Model_P features, calibration targets, imputation, model selection, or the contact transformer.

## Required GAME_SCORE_V5_STATCAST feature family

The game scoring model must consume the legacy sportsbook-independent history/context features plus all of:

- off_xwoba
- off_xba
- off_barrel_rate
- off_hard_hit_rate
- off_avg_exit_velocity
- opp_sp_xwoba_allowed
- opp_sp_xba_allowed
- opp_sp_barrel_rate_allowed
- opp_sp_hard_hit_rate_allowed
- opp_sp_avg_exit_velocity_allowed

These names must be in the serialized artifact's actual `run_features` array. The artifact must also declare `SPORTSEDGE_STATCAST_V1`, `statcast_consumed_by_model=true`, and the exact frozen contact-transformer SHA-256. Metadata without actual feature-column membership is a hard failure.

## Required NRFI_V5_STATCAST feature family

The first-inning model must consume the legacy first-inning history/context features plus all of:

- away_top_order_xwoba
- home_top_order_xwoba
- away_top_order_barrel_rate
- home_top_order_barrel_rate
- away_sp_xwoba_allowed
- home_sp_xwoba_allowed
- away_sp_hard_hit_rate_allowed
- home_sp_hard_hit_rate_allowed

Historical top-order identity is derived from the first three unique batters in the first inning before any current-game Statcast outcomes are applied. Live top-order identity must come from the resolved projected/confirmed batting order. Missing lineup identity, probable starter identity, or required prior Statcast evidence blocks scoring.

## Prior-only shrinkage policy

Sparse prior histories may be shrunk only toward training-period global means learned from 2021-2023. Frozen pseudo-sample sizes are: team offense 75 batted balls, pitcher allowed 50, batter 30. No 2024/2025/2026 target information may alter these priors.

## Frozen model family and gates

- Run model: HistGradientBoostingRegressor, Poisson loss, fixed hyperparameters from this protocol implementation; no 2025 tuning.
- First-inning model: HistGradientBoostingClassifier with 2024-only Platt calibration.
- Score-distribution alpha/shared-sigma selection: 2024 only on the same frozen grid used by the V4 rebuild lineage.
- Final 2025 acceptance thresholds, declared before scoring:
  - MONEYLINE aggregate calibration `z <= 2.5`.
  - Every evaluated RUN_LINE aggregate calibration bucket `z <= 2.5`.
  - Every evaluated TOTALS aggregate calibration bucket `z <= 2.5`.
  - NRFI/YRFI overall calibration `z <= 2.5` and every holdout probability bucket with at least 30 games `z <= 3.0`.
  - At least 1,000 valid 2025 game rows and at least 1,000 valid 2025 first-inning rows must survive all identity/Statcast gates.

A failure of any required market gate prevents V5 deployment for that market. No tolerance may be widened after seeing 2025.

## HITS / TOTAL_BASES direction

Future Statcast-aware hitter artifacts must explicitly consume batter xwOBA, xBA, barrel rate, hard-hit rate and average exit velocity plus opposing-starter allowed contact quality. Existing Hits/TB artifacts are not relabeled or self-promoted by this protocol.

## Fail-closed rules

1. Legacy artifacts without the Statcast contract cannot emit official V5 Model_P.
2. A claimed Statcast artifact whose actual model feature contract omits any required Statcast feature is rejected.
3. Missing/non-numeric required live Statcast inputs block scoring.
4. Statcast fetch failure, cutoff ambiguity, player/team binding ambiguity, lineup ambiguity, or probable-starter ambiguity blocks scoring.
5. A missing/mismatched frozen contact-transformer SHA blocks scoring.
6. Sportsbook probability/price contamination remains prohibited from Model_P.
7. No deployment eligibility change is allowed until V5 independently passes historical holdout, artifact hashing, live feature parity, runtime attestation and Truth Gate tests.

## Acceptance evidence required before deployment

- Exact source-range/cutoff tests for historical raw Statcast rows.
- Exact SHA-256 of the frozen expected-contact transformer.
- Feature-contract tests proving required Statcast columns are in the actual model matrix.
- Untouched 2025 holdout under the frozen thresholds above.
- Exact V5 artifact SHA-256 hashes.
- Live-slate attestation showing required Statcast values were fetched, bound, cut off correctly, generated by the frozen transformer, and consumed by the same model contract.
- Explicit failure tests proving legacy V4, missing/malformed Statcast, mismatched transformer identity and sportsbook-contaminated features cannot produce official V5 probabilities.

Until every relevant gate passes, the Statcast-required production state is BLOCKED rather than silently falling back to V4.

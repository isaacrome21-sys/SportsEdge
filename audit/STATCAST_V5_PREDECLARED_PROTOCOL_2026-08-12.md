# SportsEdge MLB Statcast-required V5 protocol — 2026-08-12

## Purpose

Statcast must be an actual predictive input, not presentation context. Existing GAME_SCORE_V4_CUTOFF_CORRECT and NRFI_V4_CUTOFF_CORRECT remain valid for their prior validation claims, but they are not Statcast models and may not be represented as such.

## Frozen chronology

- Upstream expected-contact transformer pretraining: 2018-2020 regular season only.
- V5 game/NRFI training: 2021-2023 regular season only.
- Calibration/model selection: 2024 only.
- Final untouched historical holdout: 2025 only.
- 2026 labels are prohibited from fitting, calibration, hyperparameter selection, threshold tuning, priors, or feature-transformer fitting.
- For each target date, all game-result state and rolling Statcast-derived state must come from dates strictly before the target date. Same-day completed games may not update another game on the same official date.

The upstream transformer window ends before downstream V5 training starts. Therefore no 2021-2025 target-game outcome can enter its own expected-contact transform through transformer fitting.

## Point-in-time Statcast policy

Historical Savant expected-stat columns are not trusted as point-in-time snapshots because retrospective recomputation/revision cannot be ruled out from a current historical pull. V5 therefore does not consume current historical `estimated_woba_using_speedangle`, `estimated_ba_using_speedangle`, `estimated_slg_using_speedangle`, or equivalent expected-stat columns.

The expected-contact layer is frozen separately before downstream V5 fitting:

1. Pull raw official Savant plate-appearance/event data for 2018-2020.
2. Use raw `launch_speed` and `launch_angle` as the transformer inputs.
3. Define hard hit deterministically as `launch_speed >= 95` mph.
4. Define barrel from the raw Savant `launch_speed_angle == 6` classification.
5. Fit a frozen contact transformer on 2018-2020 only. The hit target is 1 for single/double/triple/home_run and 0 for other tracked contact outcomes. The fixed contact-value target is out/error/FC=0.00, single=0.90, double=1.25, triple=1.60, home_run=2.00.
6. Hash and serialize that transformer separately. Every 2021-2025/live expected-contact feature is generated by that frozen transformer; the downstream V5 artifacts must record its SHA-256.

The resulting `xwoba` feature labels in the V5 contract mean SportsEdge frozen expected contact value on this fixed wOBA-like scale. They are not represented as MLB's retrospectively generated Savant xwOBA value.

## Permitted sources

- Official MLB StatsAPI for schedule, identity, results, probable pitchers, lineups/rosters and non-Statcast game facts.
- Official MLB Baseball Savant / Statcast event data for raw contact inputs and the raw launch-speed-angle barrel classification.
- Sportsbook data is price-only and prohibited from Model_P features, calibration targets, imputation, model selection, or the contact transformer.

## Required GAME_SCORE_V5_STATCAST feature family

The game scoring model must consume the legacy sportsbook-independent history/context features plus all of:

- off_xwoba
- off_xba
- off_barrel_rate
- off_hard_hit_rate
- off_avg_exit_velocity
- opp_sp_xwoba_allowed
- opp_sp_xba_allowed
- opp_sp_barrel_rate_allowed
- opp_sp_hard_hit_rate_allowed
- opp_sp_avg_exit_velocity_allowed

These names must be in the serialized artifact's actual `run_features` array. The artifact must also declare `SPORTSEDGE_STATCAST_V1`, `statcast_consumed_by_model=true`, and the exact frozen contact-transformer SHA-256. Metadata without actual feature-column membership is a hard failure.

## Required NRFI_V5_STATCAST feature family

The first-inning model must consume the legacy first-inning history/context features plus all of:

- away_top_order_xwoba
- home_top_order_xwoba
- away_top_order_barrel_rate
- home_top_order_barrel_rate
- away_sp_xwoba_allowed
- home_sp_xwoba_allowed
- away_sp_hard_hit_rate_allowed
- home_sp_hard_hit_rate_allowed

Historical top-order identity is reconstructed from the first three unique batters in target-game PA order before any current-game outcomes update state. This uses target-game player identity/order only, not target-game Statcast outcome values. Live top-order identity must come from the resolved projected/confirmed batting order. Missing lineup identity, starter identity, or required prior Statcast evidence blocks scoring.

## Frozen sample gates

No production mean-imputation is permitted. Before a row can score, prior rolling evidence must meet all of these minimums:

- team offense: 75 prior batted-ball events;
- opposing starting pitcher: 30 prior batted-ball events allowed;
- each top-order batter used by NRFI/YRFI: 20 prior batted-ball events.

Rows below a minimum are blocked. These minimums may not be lowered after seeing 2025.

## Frozen model family and gates

- Run model: HistGradientBoostingRegressor, Poisson loss, fixed implementation hyperparameters; no 2025 tuning.
- First-inning model: HistGradientBoostingClassifier with 2024-only Platt calibration.
- Score-distribution alpha/shared-sigma selection: 2024 only on the same frozen grid used by the V4 rebuild lineage.
- Final 2025 acceptance thresholds, declared before scoring:
  - MONEYLINE aggregate calibration `z <= 2.5`.
  - Every evaluated RUN_LINE aggregate calibration bucket `z <= 2.5`.
  - Every evaluated TOTALS aggregate calibration bucket `z <= 2.5`.
  - NRFI/YRFI overall calibration `z <= 2.5` and every holdout probability bucket with at least 30 games `z <= 3.0`.
  - At least 1,000 valid 2025 game rows and at least 1,000 valid 2025 first-inning rows must survive all identity/Statcast gates.

A failure of any required market gate prevents V5 deployment for that market. No tolerance may be widened after seeing 2025.

## HITS / TOTAL_BASES direction

Future Statcast-aware hitter artifacts must explicitly consume batter expected-contact value/xBA, barrel rate, hard-hit rate and average exit velocity plus opposing-starter allowed contact quality. Existing Hits/TB artifacts are not relabeled or self-promoted by this protocol.

## Fail-closed rules

1. Legacy artifacts without the Statcast contract cannot emit official V5 Model_P.
2. A claimed Statcast artifact whose actual model feature contract omits any required Statcast feature is rejected.
3. Missing/non-numeric required live Statcast inputs block scoring.
4. Statcast fetch failure, cutoff ambiguity, player/team binding ambiguity, lineup ambiguity, or probable-starter ambiguity blocks scoring.
5. A missing/mismatched frozen contact-transformer SHA blocks scoring.
6. Sportsbook probability/price contamination remains prohibited from Model_P.
7. Current historical Savant expected-stat columns are prohibited from V5 feature generation.
8. No deployment eligibility change is allowed until V5 independently passes historical holdout, artifact hashing, live feature parity, runtime attestation and Truth Gate tests.

## Acceptance evidence required before deployment

- Exact source-range/cutoff tests for historical raw Statcast rows.
- Exact SHA-256 of the frozen 2018-2020 expected-contact transformer.
- Feature-contract tests proving required Statcast columns are in the actual model matrix.
- Untouched 2025 holdout under the frozen thresholds above.
- Exact V5 artifact SHA-256 hashes.
- Live-slate attestation showing required Statcast values were fetched, bound, cut off correctly, generated by the frozen transformer, and consumed by the same model contract.
- Explicit failure tests proving legacy V4, missing/malformed Statcast, mismatched transformer identity, forbidden Savant expected-stat substitution and sportsbook-contaminated features cannot produce official V5 probabilities.

Until every relevant gate passes, the Statcast-required production state is BLOCKED rather than silently falling back to V4.

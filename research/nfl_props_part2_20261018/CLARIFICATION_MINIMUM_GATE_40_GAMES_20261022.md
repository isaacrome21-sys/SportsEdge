## The clarification

1. In all three documents the gate is phrased "no verdict review before … 40 distinct games per market." That number is a **minimum review gate**: it sets when a verdict review may happen, not where the sample ends. **Rows beyond 40 games are included.**
2. "Exactly" denotes the **precision of the threshold** — Part 2B §4 defines its own exact-number rule in the same sentence: "every threshold in this document is an exact number, no approximations." 39 games is not 40 and is not rounded up. It is not a cap, and no document contains a truncation or first-40-window rule.
3. **The one-review rule (owner's line, verbatim):** "The verdict review happens once, at the first slate close where all gates are met. The rows graded at that point are the sample of record, and no review is rerun at a later count unless a new version preregisters it."

## Effect

This memo interprets the frozen text; it changes no gate, threshold, or scoring rule, and it applies to the clocks already running under all three documents. A market that reaches its gates is reviewed exactly once, on the sample of record at that moment.
